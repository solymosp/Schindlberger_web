Coaching

Coaching with an engineering mindset

The goal of coaching is to develop employees' leadership skills. One of the most important drivers of staff turnover is a lack of genuine leadership capability in appointed managers, and the resulting tense workplace atmosphere being left unaddressed. Over time this leads to rolling turnover, which brings loss of expertise, damage to reputation and constant overload of individual organisational units.

Leadership is a skill whose desired level is built through development and experience. A coach has a unique opportunity to work through questions with a manager that the daily routine of the normal working environment usually leaves no room for. A successful coaching process has a multiplier effect: the results achieved during coaching filter into the daily work of management, and through it into the daily work of their team members.

We particularly recommend coaching:

  • To support key engineers whose role depends heavily on the ability to organise within the company's field of competing interests, and to get things done without a line management position (project engineers, lead developers, process engineers, technical team leaders).
  • To support line managers. Typical goals include facilitating decision-making ability (willingness and speed), developing group management skills, handling and where possible reducing perceived workplace stress, and – through the manager's development – increasing the job satisfaction (loyalty) of their team members.


For coaching processes, we provide coaches who have real leadership experience and are successful in their own fields.
