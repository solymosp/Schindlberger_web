Quality management methods

We teach the quality management methods customary in car manufacturing, and also offer simplified training in them for other segments of the vehicle industry and for other manufacturing companies where these methodologies can be implemented well. The training places strong emphasis on practical usability and on developing the ability to embed the methods in everyday practice. Our courses follow course material reviewed by a licensed certification auditor, and standards training is always delivered by a licensed certification auditor.
