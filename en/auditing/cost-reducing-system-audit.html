auditing

Cost-reducing system audits

We audit automotive (IATF 16949:2016) and general (ISO 9001:2015) quality management systems with the aim of optimising the system and saving costs. Beyond assessing compliance with the requirements of the standard, our goal is to identify improvement potential that delivers meaningful simplification and long-term savings at system level.

1

The audits provide an opportunity to present and discuss industry best-practice solutions.

2

On request, we make specific recommendations on cost-reduction potential wherever it can be clearly identified.

3

Our auditors can also help you close identified nonconformities efficiently.

Supplier development

As a third party, we take part in supplier management, primarily in quality improvement tasks and in selection assessments.

Entering the automotive industry

We assess companies that wish to enter the automotive sector. We can help them understand the applicable requirements, assess the activities that need to be built up, and provide training and mentoring for them.

Auditor training

We train and mentor internal auditors on the basis of the IATF 16949:2016 and ISO 9001:2015 standards. Professional preparation is always supervised by a certification auditor.
