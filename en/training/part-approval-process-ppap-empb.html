PPAP - EMPB
Production part approval process

Course description

At the end of this one-day course, participants will be able to plan a part sampling process. They will understand the concept of submission levels and how they are determined, as well as the exact content and format requirements of the documents to be submitted. In the second half of the module we cover in detail how changes after approval must be handled. Professionals who complete the course will be able to compile a high-quality initial sample package and give a professional assessment of a submitted package. (The course covers the procedures of the PPAP manual and assumes basic familiarity with production quality methods. If this is not in place and/or the procedure according to VDA requirements also needs to be reviewed, the training requires at least 2 days.)

Recommended add-ons

VDA 2 add-on PPA (PPF) +1 day
On the additional training day we discuss the requirements of the German Association of the Automotive Industry (VDA) for product and process approval. Beyond planning the sampling, participants receive detailed information on sampling levels, the documents to be submitted and how to handle changes after the start of series production.
