About us

Schindlberger Consulting is a professional association with German roots, specialising primarily in the automotive industry and in supporting automotive quality management. Building on our experience, our goal is to provide genuine, market-proven expertise that contributes lastingly to the success of the region and to improving companies' operating results.

As invited lecturers, we have taught quality management in the ELTE MBA programme and have helped educate the engineers of the future in the automotive programme of Széchenyi István University in Győr.
